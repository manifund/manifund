# Milestone values

Version: `milestones-2026-11-20b` (Caroline's values). Snapshot of the
[Manifund AI Safety Milestone Values sheet](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1utsGshqF2W_DCdQIqp6KvM9nyAEuiIq5SaoeLvwulek/edit).
When the sheet changes, re-export it here and bump the version, in this file and in `lib.ts`.

**Anchor:** a pause with China, with compute verification and a global alignment sprint (as in the AI Futures
Project's Plan A) = **1,000,000 points**, fixed by fiat. Everything below is in the same units. Negative numbers are
in parentheses: the milestone is judged net harmful.

**$/point** is the realized cost to about Oct 2026 divided by the points, from the sheet's "Cost per point" tab.
Most costs are order-of-magnitude guesses, and every priced row is a success picked with hindsight. So these figures
are an optimistic bound for what money can buy, not a typical rate. A typical grant should buy fewer points per
dollar than the median row. Blank means not priced: hypothetical, not yet achieved, or no separable cost.
